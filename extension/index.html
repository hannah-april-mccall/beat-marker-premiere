02Markers
Clip markers read ♩ 1, ♩ 2… Beat 1 is green; other beats are blue. Beats are saved across the source song, so trimming reveals the right beats. Other uses of the same song share its markers. Choose every beat, bar starts, or one beat number.

Removes this tool’s beat markers only from the selected music clips. Your own markers are kept. Other uses of the same source song share its clip markers. Undo restores the last marker change.

03Align edits to markers
Fits video clips between the music clips’ beat markers, using each song’s current position and trims. Matching linked audio follows; the music and markers stay fixed. Preview checks source handles and neighbouring clips. Beat positions round to the nearest video frame.

Reading the active timeline…

The clip can move on the timeline. Start preserves its source in-point and changes the tail; End preserves its source out-point and changes the head. Midpoint adjusts both around the source centre, within half a frame. Time stretch / shrink keeps the current source in/out points and changes playback speed. The chosen source start, centre or end anchors the fit to nearby beats.
Maximum distance each clip edge may move to reach a beat. In ripple mode this is measured after earlier clips have shifted, so the total shift can accumulate. Clips without a suitable beat pair are reported as skipped.
Length and position changes shift later clips on the chosen video track and their matching linked audio. Existing gaps are carried forward. Other tracks, music and beat markers stay fixed.
